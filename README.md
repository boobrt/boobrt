### Hi, I'm Bob Girardi

Product manager building AI agents. I built the project below during Product Faculty's AI Product Management course (2026), taking one idea from problem to a tested, working agent.

**Featured: [Workout Program Manager](https://github.com/boobrt/workout-program-manager)**
An AI agent that keeps a training plan on track when life gets in the way. It reads the athlete's calendar, plan and training data, proposes what to move, shorten, replace or skip, refuses medical calls, and changes nothing until the athlete approves.
[Live prototype](https://boobrt.github.io/workout-program-manager/) · [PRD](https://github.com/boobrt/workout-program-manager/blob/main/docs/PRD.md) · [Eval results: 5 of 5 after three rounds](https://github.com/boobrt/workout-program-manager/blob/main/docs/eval-final-2026-10-25.md)

**How I work**
- Start from a real workflow and one measurable outcome (here, planned sessions kept).
- Keep people in control: the agent proposes, the person approves.
- Test with cases built to break it, judge the results myself, and fix what fails.
